# XP Play 8-bit round theme ideas

Each theme should ship as one coherent pack: a bright main background, a quieter player background, readable answer panels, a pixel font pairing, accents, a dedicated break scene, a dedicated finale scene, and matching contextual copy.

## Built theme packs

1. **Retro Sports** — a broad multisport arena with football, rugby, cricket, tennis, basketball, badminton, golf and cue-sports equipment. Break: “Time out”. Finale: “Full time”.
2. **Pixel Cinema** — cinema curtains, marquee bulbs, film reels, clapperboards and a premiere-night scoreboard. Break: “Intermission”. Finale: “Roll credits”.
3. **Synthwave Music Festival** — pixel stages, equaliser towers, cassette motifs, lasers and crowd silhouettes. Break: “Set change”. Finale: “Encore”.
4. **World Tour** — airport departure boards, passports, pixel landmarks and map routes. Break: “Next stop”. Finale: “Journey complete”.
5. **Deep Sea Discovery** — submarine windows, coral, sonar displays and bioluminescent creatures. Break: “Surface interval”. Finale: “Expedition complete”.
6. **Detective Noir** — rainy neon streets, evidence boards, case files and a magnifying-glass reveal. Break: “Review the clues”. Finale: “Case closed”.

## Further possibilities

- **Time Travellers** — a pixel portal joining prehistoric, medieval and future eras.
- **Mad Science Lab** — bubbling tanks, coils, warning lights and experiment readouts.
- **Mega Arcade** — cabinet rows, tokens, high-score tables and boss-battle reveals.
- **Jungle Expedition** — vines, temple ruins, relics and treasure-map progress.
- **Pirate Voyage** — ship decks, maps, sea monsters and treasure-chest scoreboards.
- **Food Fight** — colourful diner signs, kitchen counters, ingredients and recipe cards.
- **Ancient Worlds** — mosaics, museum displays, ruins and mythic artefacts.
- **Comic Heroes** — city skylines, halftone panels, speech balloons and spotlight reveals.
- **Winter Lodge** — snowglobes, ski-lodge warmth, fairy lights and frosted score panels.
- **Spooky Carnival** — striped tents, ghost trains, fortune booths and eerie fairground bulbs.
- **Pixel Nature Trail** — forests, mountains, wildlife badges and campsite intermissions.
- **Around the UK** — railway boards, landmarks, seaside arcades and regional motifs.

These six additions cover sport, film, music, geography, science and mystery rounds without replacing the existing Quiz Show, Western, Sci-Fi, Fantasy, Monster and Celebration packs.
